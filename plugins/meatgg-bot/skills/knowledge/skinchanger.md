# Skinchanger

- Skins are picked on the site, at `https://meat.gg/skinchanger/weapons`, and not in game.
- After picking them, the player writes `!wp` in game chat to load the loadout onto the server.
  A pick made mid round applies on the next weapon pickup or the next round.
- The StatTrak kill count is set on the same page, next to the skin. The old `!stb` command is
  gone and no longer does anything.
- Skins are visual and live on meat.gg servers only. They do not appear in the Steam inventory
  and do not transfer to other servers.
