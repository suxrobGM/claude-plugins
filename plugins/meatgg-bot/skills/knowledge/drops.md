# Drops

- **Daily skin.** Earned by playtime on the servers, one per day. Once won it waits on the
  drops page and has to be claimed within 24 hours.
- **Weekly case.** A Premium feature, one per week, claimed within 72 hours.
- An unclaimed drop expires when its window runs out, and expiring is not a fault of the site.
- **Before claiming** the account needs a working Steam trade link, a public inventory and an
  active Steam Guard mobile authenticator. The drops page checks the first two and shows what
  is wrong; a missing authenticator is what the market rejects the purchase over.
- **After claiming** the market bot buys the item and sends a Steam trade offer. The offer
  lives for 5 minutes, so a player who claims and walks away loses it.
- A drop that ended FAILED or EXPIRED can be handed back to the player, with a fresh claim
  window and nothing bought until they claim it again. Nothing is bought twice.
- A drop is an item, not money. There is no payout, no swap for another skin and no
  compensation for a missed window.
