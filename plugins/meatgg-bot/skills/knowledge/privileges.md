# Premium

- Premium is the only purchasable subscription on the site. Prices in rubles: 300 for 7 days,
  500 for 14 days, 800 for 30 days.
- Terms are fixed at 7, 14 or 30 days. Premium is never permanent, and buying again extends
  from the current expiry rather than replacing it.
- Premium can be bought as a gift for another account.
- Nothing is given away for free, and there is no trial period.
- Whether a specific account has it, and until when, comes from `get_subscription`. The weekly
  case is a Premium feature, so an account without Premium never gets one.
- Game VIP sold in the shop is a separate purchase from the site Premium.
